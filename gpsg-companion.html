Dice Roller

Enter dice in XdY form (e.g. 1d100, 3d6+2). Modifier +/- optional.
Dice engine: integer dice, random using crypto when available.

Character Creation & Sheet

Follow rules: base 25 in each attribute; distribute 40 points; can reduce up to 4 attributes to 20; no attribute above 40 pre-XP; starting XP 2000. Skills/talents costs available in XP tab.

Attributes

Base: 25 each. Extra 40 points to distribute. Optionally reduce up to 4 attributes to 20 to re-distribute their points. No attribute > 40 before spending XP.
Points to distribute: 40
Reductions used: 0/4
Starting XP: 2000

Languages & Starting Talent

Languages = IntBonus - 1. Each language is a "Language" talent with rank 4. You may trade a language for any skill at rank 2 but must keep at least 1 language.

Derived Stats

Combat Simulator

Simulate an attack: roll attack d100, resolve hit location (ones digit), apply damage, compute reductions and special head effect.

Target body parts health

Head extra: if damage after bonuses > EndBonus, add extra 1d10 to head damage. Damage reduction: floor(EndBonus / 2)

Percentile Tests

Roll two d10s to simulate percentile rolls. Enter a Target Number (TN) to calculate success.
